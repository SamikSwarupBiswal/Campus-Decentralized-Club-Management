# Campus DAO Design System

## Color

- Canvas: `#f4f5f0` — warm, low-glare workspace background.
- Surface: `#ffffff` — primary cards and navigation.
- Ink: `#17221f` — high-contrast body and display text.
- Muted: `#68736f` — supporting text and table metadata.
- Line: `#dfe4df` — quiet boundaries.
- Forest: `#173f35` — primary action and active navigation.
- Mint: `#d7f36b` — sparingly used signal accent.
- Positive: `#237a57`; Warning: `#ad6b16`; Negative: `#ad3e3e`.

## Typography

- Display: Newsreader, editorial and civic in tone.
- Interface/body: Manrope, compact and highly legible.
- Data: Geist Mono for addresses, hashes, and transaction identifiers.

## Layout

- Desktop uses a fixed 248 px rail and a fluid content canvas.
- Main content max-width is 1480 px with 24–32 px gutters.
- Cards use 18–24 px internal spacing and 18 px corner radius.
- Mobile collapses the rail into a compact top bar and bottom navigation.

## Components

- Buttons are pill-shaped or 12 px rounded, with explicit focus rings.
- Cards use a 1 px line plus a restrained ambient shadow.
- Status appears as a dot plus text, never color alone.
- Data tables become stacked, readable cards on narrow screens.
- Accent mint is reserved for network/live signals and primary chart emphasis.
